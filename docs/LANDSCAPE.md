# Landscape — what exists, what other hobbies teach us, and what SunTzu RC should be

Status: **research, 2026-10-23.** Why SunTzu RC exists: three RC enthusiasts want the hobby to grow, with a genuinely useful, well-made platform that people can take part in. This file looks at what already serves RC pilots and racers, how platforms in other hobbies helped those hobbies grow, and turns both into principles for SunTzu RC. Figures were read from each product's own pages on 2026-10-23 unless noted; most growth numbers are the companies' own.

## What exists in RC

**Nobody offers a modern, verified, free events + places + community platform across disciplines.** The field is split three ways:

| Kind                                 | Products                                                                                                                                   | Strong at                                                                                                                           | Weak at                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Official bodies, one discipline each | AMA (246 US events, Club Finder with 2,417 clubs), BMFA (UK), AMYA (boats), MultiGP RaceSync (FPV, ~500 chapters, app rated 4.9)           | Authority; BMFA's ICS/Google/Outlook subscription, free organiser dashboard and "request a trial flight"; RaceSync's race-day tools | 10 events per page, no map, no "near me"; each stays inside its discipline and country |
| Racing tools                         | RCSignup (~61 events), LiveRC (177 tracks, live timing), MyRCM, RC Scoring Pro, Speedhive, FPVScores                                       | Entry, timing, results; LiveRC's "racing now" status; embeddable scoreboards                                                        | Not calendars; no map or search by place; cars or FPV only                             |
| Club websites                        | DualRates ($179/yr, built around how a club runs), Wix, WordPress                                                                          | Each club's own calendar and membership                                                                                             | Nothing connects clubs to each other                                                   |
| Indie apps                           | **BashMaps** (closest to SunTzu RC: map, events, clubs, feed, all disciplines; 54 iOS ratings), RC Flyer, aeromura, Spot&Fly, RC Event Hub | Spots added by users, "flying now" check-ins, reviews                                                                               | App-only (invisible to Google), unverified posts, BashMaps charges to create events    |

Communities are big and growing (r/fpv 186k members, +22 % in a year; r/rccars 166k; r/RCPlanes 72k, +26 %) but scattered across Reddit, Facebook groups, forums and small Discords. "RC track near me" searches return mostly spam.

**The gaps SunTzu RC can fill:**

1. One verified calendar across air, cars, FPV and boats.
2. A map and "near me" for events, not only for clubs.
3. Real web pages Google can index: the indie apps live only inside apps.
4. Trust you can see: sources and a "last checked" date. Nobody shows them.
5. Free, easy publishing for organisers (BashMaps charges, Facebook is closed, DualRates stays inside one club).
6. Change and cancellation alerts across all sources.
7. A path from "curious" to "first day" for every discipline, not just planes.
8. The event page keeps living after the day, linked to its results.

## What other hobbies teach us

| Platform                 | What made it work                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| UDisc (disc golf)        | 17,287 courses, 21M rounds logged a year. Free league and event tools; volunteer Course Ambassadors who must review their course yearly; a "was the info right?" question after every round; clubs use play counts to win new courses from councils |
| Geocaching               | ~400 volunteer reviewers check every listing against public rules; they recruit the next reviewers. Events for newcomers are a listing type of their own                                                                                            |
| Pickleheads (pickleball) | Became USA Pickleball's official court finder; ~2,200 ambassadors keep courts and schedules current; free organiser tools                                                                                                                           |
| MotorsportReg            | Sends entrants to clubs (a monthly "upcoming nearby events" email drove 3.1 % of registrations), so clubs end up paying nothing                                                                                                                     |
| RunSignup                | Free for races; race websites, email and volunteer tools included                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| NASA Night Sky Network   | 400+ astronomy clubs, 68,000+ public events. Clubs stay listed by holding and logging 5 public events a year; the public finds events by ZIP code                                                                                                   |
| eBird / iNaturalist      | Automatic filters flag the unusual; people review only those. Consensus decides what is "research grade"                                                                                                                                            |
| XContest (paragliding)   | A new takeoff can only be added with a flight from it: evidence before data                                                                                                                                                                         |
| parkrun                  | A free event at the same time and place every week grew from 13 runners to 2,500+ locations                                                                                                                                                         |
| Merlin → eBird           | A simple helper app brings people in (9.6M new Merlin users in 2025), and some become contributors                                                                                                                                                  |

**What went wrong elsewhere:** Meetup charging members per RSVP (outcry, people left for WhatsApp); Mountain Project's owner taking down an open copy of the community's climbing data; Strava's heatmap and fishing apps exposing places people wanted kept private; Paragliding Earth's site list going stale and offline with no one responsible; iNaturalist and parkrun changing what contributors valued without asking them.

## Principles for SunTzu RC

1. **Free to take part, forever.** Following, "going", alerts and listing an event never cost anything. Money, when it comes, comes from optional extras (shop listings, premium tools), never from participation.
2. **Organisers get something back for publishing with us:** a free event page, a calendar feed and a widget for their own site, and a monthly report ("views, followers, going"). That is how events from Facebook groups reach SunTzu RC: the organiser chooses to publish here, because it brings people.
3. **Trust is visible.** Every event shows its sources and when it was last checked. Events publish automatically when sources agree; changes, conflicts and new places go to the crew. After an event: one tap, "was this right?".
4. **Every place has a steward:** a club officer or track owner who reviews it yearly, with a "last verified" date. A new field needs evidence (a club page, an FAA FRIA record, photos or an event held there). Private fields are never listed without permission, and airspace and temporary flight restrictions are shown.
5. **A real path for newcomers in every discipline:** a "try it" request (ZIP code and discipline go to a nearby club, track or shop), events flagged as beginner-friendly, the guides, and a "Can I fly here?" check as the simple helper that brings people in.
6. **Data that helps the hobby, not just us:** attendance and interest per field help clubs defend their fields with parks departments and landowners. Facts (dates, places) are published under an open licence with credit to contributors.
7. **On the open web first:** every event, club and field is a fast page that Google understands. An app comes later and uses the same API.
8. **Rhythm builds habit:** recurring sessions shown as series, a weekly "this weekend near you", and one day a year when the whole Florida hobby opens its fields to the public.
9. **Ask before changing what contributors value**, and write the rules down where everyone can read them.

## What this changes in the plan

Discovery (`DISCOVERY.md`) stays, kept lean: enough coverage of the important events, correct information, and one simple estimate of what we miss. The order of work moves towards what people see and organisers use:

1. Events from the database on the web, with a page per event Google understands, a map and "near me", and a calendar to subscribe to.
2. The organiser kit: claim a club or track, publish an event by pasting a link or flyer, the embeddable widget, the monthly report (accounts and roles need Itaca engineering).
3. Places with stewards, the "try it" request and "Can I fly here?".
4. The engine keeps growing underneath: the Parallel sweep, platform readers, results linked back to events.
